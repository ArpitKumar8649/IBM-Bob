# 🎬 Writer's Room — Phased Enhancement Plan
**IBM AI Builders July Challenge · Creative Industries**

_Synthesized from a 6-dimension, 73-finding multi-agent audit of the /workspaces/IBM-Bob repo. Today: July 21, 2026 · Submission deadline: July 31, 2026 (10 days)._

## Executive summary

The repo is in a strong-concept, weak-execution state with three blockers that could sink the submission outright: the default shipped model is Llama/Qwen not Granite (challenge-fit), only README.md is tracked in git (no public code), and there is no deploy path to a public demo. The good news is the highest-leverage fixes are cheap (S effort): switching to Granite, fixing the README, committing the tree, and a one-command compose deploy move the largest combined judging score. The core innovation — the streaming multi-agent debate on a spatial canvas — exists but is invisible (synchronous invoke, no streaming wiring, only plot_beat rendered, dead Director's Cut finale), so Phases 1-2 turn a hidden mechanic into the demo's centerpiece. The plan front-loads challenge-fit and ship-blockers (Phase 0), deepens the AI crew and streams the debate (Phase 1), polishes the canvas and demo flow (Phase 2), and ships a public demo + video (Phase 3), with Liveblocks multiplayer, persistence, WebGL scale, TTS, and the full screenplay compiler reserved for post-deadline (Phase 4).
